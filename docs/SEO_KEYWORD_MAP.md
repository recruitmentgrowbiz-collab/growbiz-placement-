# SEO keyword map

Research date: 7 September 2026. Qualitative search-intent research, not search-volume or ranking measurements. India targeting is explicitly requested; city/service-office claims are not inferred.

Sources reviewed:
- [NCS job search](https://www.ncs.gov.in/job-seeker/Pages/Search.aspx): role, skill, location and experience search.
- [Naukri Campus career guidance](https://www.naukri.com/campus/career-guidance/): fresher resume and interview intent.
- [Naukri recruiter notice-period search](https://recruiterzone.naukri.com/hire-faster-with-notice-period-search/): availability is meaningful hiring context.
- [TeamLease recruitment services](https://employer.teamlease.com/recruitment-services): permanent recruitment terminology.
- [Randstad India employer solutions](https://www.randstad.in/employers/our-solutions/msp-rpo/): staffing and RPO intent.
- [Google JobPosting guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/job-posting): accurate data and vacancy lifecycle.

These sources inform vocabulary and intent only; competitor capabilities and claims are not attributed to Grow Biz. Primary targets below are editorial recommendations inferred from this research, not evidence of measured demand. Keep jobs, hiring services, campus partnerships and career guidance distinct.

## Jobs: long-tail / transactional

[Actual role] jobs in [actual location], fresher jobs by skill, hybrid IT jobs, sales vacancies, internships by location. Use existing search filters; do not create thin city/role landing pages. Job H1 stays the actual job title. Titles and descriptions use actual role, company, location and experience. Example inventory is noindex and excluded from job schema/sitemaps.

## Company pages: primary / navigational

[Company name] jobs and careers. H1 is company name; secondary terms are actual industry/location. Sections: profile facts, open roles, verification if evidenced. Links: job details and /jobs. Canonical: /companies/[actual slug]. Mock company profiles are noindex; only approved profiles should become indexable.

## Employers / recruitment services: long-tail / commercial

Hire employees for a growing team; IT and non-IT recruitment support; sales recruitment; bulk hiring requirements; contract staffing for project roles; recruitment process outsourcing. Service sections retain distinct anchors and specific scope. Do not claim local offices, guaranteed turnaround or approved fees.

## Campus: long-tail / transactional

Campus hiring for employers; institute placement-drive support; graduate recruitment; fresher internship opportunities. Separate audience sections. Placement is not guaranteed.

## Career resources: long-tail / informational

How to write a resume for jobs in India; prepare tell-me-about-yourself examples; discuss expected CTC; explain notice period to a recruiter; choose skills from job descriptions; plan career growth; prepare for a first job. Improve the existing hub with useful guides; no new article routes or mass publication.

## Career Plus: primary / commercial

Optional career support. Secondary: resume preparation, interview support, career readiness. Explicitly separate from free applications and employer decisions. Benefits/pricing availability remains subject to confirmed scope.

## Indexing rules

Marketing pages: self-canonical/index. Filter/sort/search combinations: noindex, canonical /jobs. Unfiltered valid pagination: self-canonical /jobs?page=N. Invalid pagination: not-found page and noindex. Internal/auth pages: noindex. Unapproved legal placeholders: noindex, excluded from sitemap. No service subpages or article routes exist in this app; existing service anchors and resource sections carry the content.
